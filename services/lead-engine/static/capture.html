One click sends the profile you are looking at to your own Lead Engine and returns its emails.

Can't drag? Right-click the bookmarks bar, choose "Add page", and paste the URL shown below.

3. Use it

  1. Open any profile page in the same browser.
  2. Click the bookmarklet. It reads only the fields on that page: name, company, headline, URL.
  3. You get the work email, its verification status, and any direct email or phone the engine has.
This is the same data path a commercial extension uses: the fields visible on the page you are already viewing, sent to your own account. It does not crawl, enumerate search results, or touch a profile you have not opened. Scraping at scale is what violates a site's terms, not reading one page you opened yourself.
